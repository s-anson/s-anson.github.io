About me

My name is Anson, and since the fall of 2025, I have been a PhD student in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at Columbia University. Prior to my matriculation to the program, I earned a Sc.B. in Applied Mathematics–Economics from Brown University and obtained working experience in the consulting industry. I am advised by Professor Lily Xu, and my research interests include OR methods and applications that serve social impact goals, such as disaster relief and government policies.
