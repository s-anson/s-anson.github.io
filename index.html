About me

Since the fall of 2025, I have been a PhD student in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at Columbia University, where I am advised by Professor Lily Xu.

Prior to my matriculation to the program, I earned an Sc.B. in Applied Mathematics–Economics from Brown University and worked as a management consultant. My research interests include OR methods and applications that serve social impact goals, such as disaster relief and government policies.
